How I work

From a sketch to something live.

The same five steps, whether it's a dashboard, a restaurant site or a landing page.

  1. Understand

    Who is this for, and what do they need to get done?

  2. Wireframe

    Look at the data to see what people do and what to change next.

Restaurant website, designed and built end to end

The goal
A restaurant site where people can browse the menu, order and book a table on any screen size.
Design
A warm palette taken from Indian food, category tabs so the menu stays short, and the cart total always in view at the bottom.
Build

Project 03 · Mar 2026

Amazon Customer Purchase Behaviour

Data analysis & machine learning
PythonClusteringRegression
The question
Which groups of customers exist, how much is each customer worth over time, and who is likely to stop buying?
What I did
Cleaned 2,000 customer records (missing values, outliers) and explored trends with Matplotlib and Seaborn. Then I segmented customers with K-Means, predicted Customer Lifetime Value with Linear Regression, and built a Logistic Regression churn model.
Why a designer cares
Knowing who the users are, and why they leave, is what good design decisions are built on.
